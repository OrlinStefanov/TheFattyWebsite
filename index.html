How a run goes

  1. Pick a mission in the main menu.
  2. Play the stage. Fight the zombies, open power-up chests and break the cages to free the villagers.
  3. Finish the mission. The chest screen pays out coins and gems the first time you complete it.
  4. Go to the village. The rescued villagers join it. Give them jobs, spend what they make on upgrades, then head back out. Each mission you complete moves the village forward one day.

Combat

A belt you actually swing

Move with the joystick and swing with one button. The belt is a physics rope: it whips up from under, out in front and over your head. On a hit it knocks zombies back, wraps round the target and flops off.

Spam combo

Tap fast to build heat. With heat your swings get up to 2.5× faster and the slap sound climbs in pitch. A tap during a swing chains straight into the next one. Stop tapping and it cools off.

Stamina

Running and attacking both draw from your stamina bar, so you can't just sprint and swing forever.

Day, night and flashlight

A day/night cycle lights the buildings at night, and your flashlight comes out on its own when it gets dark. Roofs fade away when you walk inside stone buildings.

Everything on screen

Health and stamina bars, a kill counter, an arrow pointing to the exit and a minimap that grows when you tap it.

Boss: the Log Brute

Waiting at the end of mission 5. It marks a spot with a red light, then slams its log down on it. After a set of slams it runs out of breath, and that is your window to hit it. Below half health it enrages: rocks rain down and Runners join the fight.

Ten missions, two regions

Forest

Missions 1–4 are rescues, and mission 5 is Clear the Forest. The zombie types show up one by one, ending with the Log Brute.

Swamp

Missions 6–9 are rescues, and mission 10 is Drain the Swamp. Expect Spitters, sunken ruins, a bog graveyard and a drowned village.

Your village

A walled village with a gate, farm, river, lumber yard, mine, forge, restaurant and market stalls. Villagers work, eat and sleep on a 24-hour clock and dress for their job.

Upgrades

  • Villagers gain XP by working. Promote them to level 2 or 3 for coins and tool kits, and stars appear over their heads.
  • Buildings: each of the 7 work buildings upgrades to level 3 for coins, wood, ore and building beams.
  • Output: every villager level above 1 adds 25%, and so does every building level, up to ×2.25 in total.

The forge

  • Upgrade yourself: Health, Belt damage, Stamina, Speed and Fart, paid for with coins, ore and wood.
  • Crafting: your blacksmiths make tool kits and building beams for village upgrades, plus the iron plate, spiked belt and running boots, which each boost your next mission only.
  • The forge's level sets how many items are made at once. More blacksmiths don't make things cheaper.

Beer party

Every 2 days at 19:00 the clock stops until you walk to the restaurant terrace. Then you get 20 seconds to tap as fast as you can and chug beers. Every beer you finish gives a random reward.

The nightly horde

A horn sounds at 20:00 and the horde attacks at 21:00, every night. Until it's beaten, you can't leave the village.

It grows

5 zombies on the first night, 2 more for every night already fought, up to 30. They come in 2–3 groups, and their types and toughness follow your furthest completed mission.

New roads

They come from the west. The south road opens after 3 nights and the north road after 6.

Raids

Zombies that reach the stores steal food, water, wood and ore in a sack. Kill them to get it back. At most a quarter of each stock can go in one night.

If you go down

You wake at 07:00 with full health. Every zombie left takes one raid's worth, within the same cap. You never lose coins or gems.

Defenses

  • Barricades: place them from the Build panel for 8 wood each, up to 20. Zombies stop and hack at them. Stand next to one to repair it.
  • Watchtowers: build them on the 11 stone pads outside the wall, up to level 3. Each level adds health, range, damage and fire rate. A tower only shoots with a Guard on it, and a higher-level guard shoots harder.

How to install

The APK isn't on Google Play, so Android will ask for permission once.

  1. Download the APK with the button above, on your phone.
  2. Open the file when the download finishes. If Android blocks it, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser.
  3. Go back and tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, choose Install anyway.
  4. Open The Fatty and play.

On Android 7.x the switch is called Unknown sources and lives in Settings → Security.
